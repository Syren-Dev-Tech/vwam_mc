# New Trades

Our modified trades are defined here.
Instead of limiting villagers to 10 trade options (2 per level), all trades per tier are available, meaning all level 1 villagers will trade for all level 1 trades.
A level 5 (master) villager will trade for everything.

We're also removing the stock amounts because trades are no longer bound to villager inventories.

## Armorer

This breaks the levels into 5 stages of production:

- Fuels and raw materials
- Iron production
- Iron gear
- Gold gear
- Diamond gear

## Cartographer

Cartographers are definitely one of the least used villagers because, especially in modded settings, maps aren't often needed for finding points of interest.
Aside from burried treasure, the only otherwise "not easily found" structure available by map is a trial chamber.
We can make this interaction with cartographers a little more worthwhile by introducing a "map exchange" mechanic, where the player is rewarded for their maps.

Therefore, we remove the trade value of POI maps and instead introduce the following process:

1. The player purchases a blank map from the cartographer, which has a "target location" to reach
2. The player must travel to that location and map the area
3. The player returns to the cartographer and returns the map
4. The cartographer rewards the player based on what points of interest are there

For example, if the target location is a forest, the value of the map would be less than if the location was a village.
The reward, though, will always be greater than the price of the blank map.

This incentivises the player to not only explore around the village, but provides a reason to visit random locations nearby, surveying the area.

As the cartographer increases in level, the "point of interest tier" of a blank map target increases.

## Cleric

Clerics will have their trade focus placed more on potions and "magic," pulling related trades from other villagers, and adding some new.
Farmers, however, will be a "partner" in these trades, given some items used in brewing would make more sense in their hands, and similarly some from farmers in the hands of clerics.

In essence, clerics will be more akin to witches.
